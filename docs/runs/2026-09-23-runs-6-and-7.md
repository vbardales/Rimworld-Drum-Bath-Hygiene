## What they turned out to be, both of the suite

1. **The order never worked.** The drum mod builds its job with target A the drum's cell and target B the drum
   (`tryGiveJob`, read from its assembly on 2026-09-23); the driver's first toil fails on a null B. The suite ordered
   `MakeJob(def, drum)`, the drum in A and B empty: the job ends inside `StartJob`, `TryTakeOrderedJob` still returns
   true, and a colonist with joy at ten per cent reaches the drum only when the drum mod's joy giver sends them,
   after 60 or 90 seconds or not within the wait. That is the spread above, and why the French capture passed at
   94 seconds of a 90-second poll plus setup. Fixed in `9df3305`; not yet re-run.
2. **The game gives a pawn its needs back when its hediffs change.** Once a French bath happened, the colonist who had
   lost the hygiene need carried it again, so the component would have bound a real clean action. The scenario now gives
   the hediff and removes the need in one step, before any tick. Fixed in `9df3305`; not yet re-run.
