## What each run showed

**Run 1** (11 scenarios). The patch lands on the hediff the game built, the gauge climbs with a control that does not,
half a bath fills an empty gauge, a bath survives a save and a reload, carried filth is cleared on the way out (the
access waiver executing), and the gauge stops when the bath does. Three failures, all of the suite:
- an animal: Pickle's own `is given hediff` finds pawns by nickname among colonists and could not find a muffalo;
- a burning drum expected `HotBath` for a healthy colonist. Dubs Bad Hygiene grants it only to a pawn with hypothermia
  (read from the IL of `SanitationUtil.WaterTempCheck`): warm water gives a healthy pawn nothing;
- a burnt-out drum expected `ColdBath`. The memory is `ColdWater`, and the report showed `ColdWater (-3)` on the pawn.

The `@review` capture, opened, showed the colonist standing beside the drum with the panel reading "Washing.", green.

**Run 2** (16). The four teleported scenarios all passed, chilled pair included. The animal failed on a
`NullReferenceException` in the drum mod's own render patch (`DrumBath_Harmony.PawnRenderer_RenderPawnAt.Prefix`), which
reads `CurJob.targetA` for a pawn that has the hediff and no job: a state given by hand, not one play produces. The
three scenarios through the drum mod's real job failed on the step after "is bathing".

**Run 3** (16). Same three. The game log then showed the whole scenario lasting 9.9 seconds: the "is bathing" step was
a `void` calling `AssertEventually` and discarding the `Task` it returns, so it never waited and could never fail. Every
use of it, including the capture's second assertion, had been vacuous. The capture was again green over a colonist far
from the drum.

**Run 4** (16). With the step awaiting, the five scenarios through the real job all timed out at about 98 seconds, the
90 of the wait plus setup, and the game log held nothing but the timeout.

**Run 5** (16, diagnostic step and a filmed capture, `ffmpeg` newly installed in the WSL). The failures were the same
five, but they now carried a job trace. It reversed the reading of run 4: **the real bath works.** The colonist walks
across the map, takes `Job_BathingAtDrumBath`, and stands on the drum's own cell (142,155) with the job running and the
hediff on for about ten seconds. The film, opened, shows her sitting in the drum with the panel reading "Relaxing in
the bath while watching the passing clouds". The step's condition had demanded `CurJob.targetA.Thing == drum`, which
the driver rewrites once it has the pawn. The animal never took its ordered job (the drum mod bathes animals through
its own component).
